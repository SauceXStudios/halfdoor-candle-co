Made in a garage, on purpose

Half Door started when I couldn't find a candle that smelled like rain on the driveway. So I made one, then eleven more. Every batch is poured on Tuesday nights and cured for two weeks before it ships.

Candle care: First time you light it, let it burn about two hours so the wax melts edge to edge. Trim the wick to a quarter inch before every burn after that.
